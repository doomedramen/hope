# Code of Conduct

Project participants must keep discussion and collaboration respectful,
specific, and safe.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and publishing another person's
private information without permission. Do not use project access to expose
secrets or personal data.

Maintainers may edit, reject, close, or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately to `wookoouk@gmail.com`. Include links,
dates, and relevant context. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.
